Fieldnotes is a small publication about making things. Furniture, pots, pages, programs: we are less interested in the finished object than in what the work of getting there teaches the person doing it.

We publish slowly, a few essays a month, written by people who make things for a living and edited by someone who reads every sentence aloud. There are no listicles, no hot takes and no advertising.

## What we look for

- **A particular workshop.** We would rather read about one kiln, one chair or one ninety-line program than about craft in general.
- **A change of mind.** The best pieces describe something the writer used to believe.
- **Plain sentences.** If a paragraph would embarrass you read aloud to a friend, it will not survive the edit.

## Writing for us

We read everything sent to us and reply to all of it, though not quickly. Send a paragraph about the thing you make and the thing it taught you. We will take it from there.
